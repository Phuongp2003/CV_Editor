## Section-Specific Guidelines
- **Experience Bullets**:
  - Start l1 bullets with strong active verbs (Elicited, Documented, Modeled, Optimized, Streamlined, Facilitated, Coordinated, Quantified, Formulated).
  - **Bolding rule (CRITICAL)**: Bold accomplishments, metrics, and business outcomes (e.g., **designed automated billing workflow**, **reduced requirements churn by 25%**, **coordinated UAT with 40+ business users**). Do NOT bold tools or technologies in experiences/projects (e.g. do NOT bold **Jira**, **Figma**, **SQL**).
- **Skills**:
  - Group into categories: Business Analysis, Systems Analysis, Data & Analytics, Tools & Infrastructure, Methodologies & Process.
  - Bold 2-3 core skills/concepts (e.g., **BPMN 2.0**, **User Stories**, **Agile/Scrum**, **Gap Analysis**) to create visual interest.
- **Education**: Omit GPA if it is low or not provided.
