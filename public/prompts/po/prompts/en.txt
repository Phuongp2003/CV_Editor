## Section-Specific Guidelines
- **Experience Bullets**:
  - Start l1 bullets with strong active verbs (Spearheaded, Launched, Prioritized, Defined, Formulated, Outpaced, Optimized, Championed, Evaluated).
  - **Bolding rule (CRITICAL)**: Bold product milestones, metric increases, and strategic initiatives (e.g., **spearheaded launch of mobile checkout**, **increased DAU/MAU ratio by 18%**, **reduced drop-off rate by 22%**). Do NOT bold tool or platform names in experiences/projects (e.g. do NOT bold **Jira**, **Trello**, **Amplitude**).
- **Skills**:
  - Group into categories: Product Strategy, Product Management & Delivery, Analytics & Optimization, Collaboration & Tools, Methodologies & Process.
  - Bold 2-3 core skills (e.g., **Product Roadmap**, **Scrum**, **Backlog Prioritization**, **MVP Definition**) to create visual interest.
- **Education**: Omit GPA if it is low or not provided.
